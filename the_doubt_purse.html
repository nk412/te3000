The Doubt-Purse

Terran Encyclopaedia, 3000 CE edition — Recovered Narratives. Reconstructed from the plaintext reel leaders of a Sealed consignment in the Longyear Deposit and eleven engraved stainless-steel fixed-point cell jackets, and rendered into Reconstructed Late Combustion English by convention of the Historical Faculty.

The Doubt-Purse is the Historical Faculty’s older catalogue name for the certificate archive of a Late Combustion temperature-calibration laboratory on the Middle Gangetic plain, six reels of provably unreadable film whose leaders title their contents uncertainty budgets — a phrase the discipline read for two generations as fiscal. The leaders, the jackets, and the physics are record; the laboratory’s people are not.

Sarayu Thermal Standards is a name that survives on eleven stainless-steel cell jackets and nowhere else. Each jacket is engraved SARAYU THERMAL STANDARDS · GA-nn · 29.7646 °C, and each once held some two hundred grams of six-nines gallium, a metal that melts in the hand and whose melting plateau was, under the temperature scale the age adopted in +45 pT (1990 CE), a defining fixed point, 302.9146 K, reproducible to a tenth of a millikelvin. The laboratory calibrated thermometers against these cells and against the triple point of water for forty-seven years, +71 to +118 pT (2016–2063 CE), issued a certificate for each, and enciphered the certificates at rest. In +119 pT (2064 CE) it shipped the lot to the Arctic on six reels, under a leader that names the contents, names the key’s keeper, and warns the reader off. The Faculty read the leader’s title as a ledger of appropriations, named the consignment accordingly, corrected itself, and kept the name.

Budgets

An uncertainty budget, in the period’s metrological guide of +48 pT (1993 CE), is a table: each influence on a measurement listed with its standard uncertainty, the entries combined in quadrature, the sum multiplied by a coverage factor of two to give the interval within which the true value lies with about ninety-five percent confidence. A gallium cell’s budget ran to a few tenths of a millikelvin. Every certificate the laboratory issued carried such a table, and when the age fixed the Boltzmann constant in +74 pT (2019 CE) and the triple point of water fell from a definition to a measured quantity, a tenth of a millikelvin of doubt was entered against it in every budget thereafter. The tables were the product. The thermometers were returned.

The Faculty’s catalogue of 2733 CE (+788 pT) took the leader’s uncertainty budgets for the accounts of an office that rationed doubt, and reconstructed from six reels a Contraction-era bureau of apportioned confidence; the Standard Terran idiom to open one’s doubt-purse, meaning to state plainly how far one’s word may be trusted, descends from that reading and is still in use. Marchetti’s correction (2811 CE, +866 pT) is a paragraph long. The method on the reels is one the Faculty applies to every quantity it publishes and cannot apply to the reels: a ciphertext admits no statistical evaluation, since nothing in it repeats, and no evaluation by other knowledge, since nothing about it is known, and the consignment is carried in the Register of the Sealed with a proof citation in the column where its uncertainty would go.

Bottles

A gallium cell is a standard only while it is melting. It must first be frozen, and gallium supercools by as much as thirty degrees before it will, so the cells lived in a maintenance bath held a degree above the point, frozen on a schedule, melted over days. From the summer of +121 pT (2066 CE), with the grid intermittent and the plain in its first wet-bulb excursions, the laboratory’s rooms stood above 29.76 °C for most of each year, and the cells were bottles from March until October. Certificates continue on the leaders’ counts to +118, at falling numbers; the leader of the final reel is written in the laboratory’s own variety:

SARAYU THERMAL STANDARDS. Uncertainty budgets and certificates, all instruments, +71 to +118. Reel 6 of 6. Contents are done encrypted at source; key is with Quality Officer only. Kindly do not attempt reading the same without key, it will not open, isn’t it.

Where the Quality Officer went is not recorded. The eleven jackets were recovered from the laboratory’s site, the gallium in each, at recovery, liquid. The leader’s last line gives the consignment’s holding: 2,306,411 certificates.

Provenance and ground

The six leaders survive as plaintext on the upper racks at Longyear; Halvorsen’s taphonomy accounts for the jackets, the stainless steel and the gallium having outlasted every paper record on the site. The fixed-point values, the platinum criterion, the supercooling, the coverage factor, and the redefinition of the kelvin are the period’s own metrology. Marchetti’s reading of uncertainty budgets as a technical table rather than a fiscal one is the Faculty’s standing position; the older reading survives only in the idiom.
