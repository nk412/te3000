# The Stay-Well Float

*Terran Encyclopaedia, 3000 CE edition — Recovered Narratives. Reconstructed from the cleartext cycle headers of a ciphered profile store held among the Sealed, and the engraved hull collar that carried it, and rendered into Reconstructed Late Combustion English by convention of the Historical Faculty.*

> **The Stay-Well Float is the Historical Faculty's gloss on the engraved name of a Late Combustion ocean-profiling float recovered intact, unpowered, and provably unreadable from a raised strandline of the northern Bay of Bengal.** The hull, the engraving, and the headers are record; the school that named it is reconstruction.

---

**The Stay-Well Float is an autonomous profiling float whose 258 stored ocean profiles are symmetric-ciphered against a key held only by its receiving centre, and whose cleartext cycle headers show it kept its ten-day schedule for two years after anyone acknowledged it.** Deployed 20 May +126 pT (2071 CE), its anodised aluminium collar is engraved BHALO THEKO 4B — a delta parting, *stay well*, and a class number — under a scheme by which schoolchildren named floats.

## The headers

The last acknowledged uplink is cycle 183, +131 pT (2076 CE); seventy-five headers follow, all of one shape:

> `C258 2078-06-12T03:12Z SURF PROF 2000->0 STORED TX 12/12 ACK 0 DESC 1000`

The key never left the centre; the centre left no trace.

## The char

The school stood on a tidal char, its yard 1.1 m above local datum at deployment and below it on the modern map. The ocean took nine-tenths of the heat the age retained; seawater expands on warming, about 1.3 × 10⁻⁴ K⁻¹ at the float's 1,000 dbar park depth, and that expansion supplied half the sea-level rise of +26 to +73 pT (1971–2018 CE). The steric part of the water over the schoolyard is the depth-integral of the quantity the float was built to log. Cycle 258 surfaced on 12 June +133 pT (2078 CE), attempted uplink twelve times, and descended.
